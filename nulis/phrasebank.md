# Phrase Bank — Frasa Template per Move

Sumber: model CARS (USC/Swales), Glasman-Deal, Knuth. Frasa Inggris (bahasa naskah); pakai sebagai kerangka, sesuaikan dengan substansi. Jangan tempel mentah — variasikan agar tidak terbaca generik/AI.

## Introduction — Move 1 (Establishing a Territory)
- Claiming centrality: "X has attracted considerable attention in recent years." · "Understanding X is critical to..."
- Topic generalization: "It is well established that..." · "Previous studies have consistently shown that..."
- Reviewing research: "Several authors [1]–[3] have reported..." · "Building on the work of A (2020), B (2022) demonstrated..."

## Introduction — Move 2 (Establishing a Niche)
- Indicating a gap: "However, little is known about..." · "Nevertheless, few studies have examined..." · "To date, no study has addressed..."
- Counter-claiming: "This assumption, however, may not hold when..." · "Contrary to this view,..."
- Question-raising: "It remains unclear whether..." · "An important question that has not been fully answered is..."
- Neutral contrast (tanpa merendahkan): "Prior research has focused primarily on A, rather than on B." · "Although considerable research has been devoted to A, less attention has been paid to B."
- Sinyal leksikal: kontras (however, although, yet, nevertheless) · kuantor negatif (few, little, no, none) · verba evaluatif (fail to, lack, overlook, neglect, ignore) · adjektiva (inadequate, incomplete, inconclusive, unclear, unconvincing)

## Introduction — Move 3 (Occupying the Niche)
- Announcing: "This study aims to..." · "In this paper, we propose..." · "The present work investigates..."
- Principal findings: "We show that..." · "Our results indicate that..."
- Roadmap: "The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. Section 2..."

## Methods
- Justifikasi: "X was chosen because..." · "To account for Y, we adopted..."
- Prosedur: "Participants were randomly assigned to..." · "Data were collected via..." · "The analysis was conducted using [software]."
- Kualitatif reflexivity: "As a researcher with a background in X, I..." · "To enhance trustworthiness, we employed member checking and..."

## Results
- Location: "Table 2 presents..." · "As shown in Figure 3,..."
- Highlighting: "The most notable finding is..." · "Contrary to expectations,..."
- Statistik: "There was a significant effect of X on Y, t(48) = 2.34, p = .02, d = 0.61, 95% CI [0.12, 1.09]."
- Kualitatif: "This theme was echoed by several participants. As P7 noted: '...'"

## Discussion
- Restate: "This study set out to... The findings suggest that..."
- Terhadap literatur: "These results are consistent with A (2020), who found..." · "In contrast to B (2019), we observed..., possibly because..."
- Hedging: "may", "might", "appears to", "suggests that", "is likely to", "one possible explanation is that..."
- Implikasi: "These findings have implications for..." · "Practically, this suggests that..."
- Limitasi: "This study has several limitations. First,... which may have led to [arah bias]. Nonetheless,..."
- Arah lanjut: "Future research could examine..." · "A promising direction is..."

## Conclusion
- Kontribusi: "This study contributes to... by demonstrating..." · "To our knowledge, this is the first work to..."
- Takeaway: "Taken together, these findings indicate that..."

## Matematika (Knuth/Krantz)
- Claim device: "We claim that [statement]. Assuming this for the moment, we complete the proof of the theorem; the claim is established in Section X."
- Defer: "The proof of Lemma 3, which is technical, is deferred to Section 8."
- Status: "It remains to show that..." · "We have already proved that..." · "This will not be proved here, but see [Ref]."
- Perbaikan gaya: "Consider Sₙ, where n < p." (bukan "Consider Sₙ, n < p.") · "The polynomial xⁿ − a has n distinct roots." (jangan awali dengan simbol)
